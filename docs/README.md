# Documentation

The [project README](../README.md) is the overview. These pages hold the detail:

- **[Widgets](widgets.md)**: every widget at every cell size, its options, binary sensor and domain state icons, date/time formats, and templates in labels.
- **[HTML widget](html-widget.md)**: design your own cells with HTML/CSS and Jinja, the fluid kit, and animated widgets.
- **[Layouts](layouts.md)**: all 19 layouts with their slot counts.
- **[Entities, services & automations](automation.md)**: every entity a device exposes, the `geekmagic.notify` action, and a presence-based sleep/wake automation.
- **[Device compatibility](devices/README.md)**: supported firmware profiles, per-device reports, and how to capture a report for an unsupported device.
- **[Development](development.md)**: the local Home Assistant (`scripts/dev-ha`), regenerating samples and screenshots, and testing against a real device.
